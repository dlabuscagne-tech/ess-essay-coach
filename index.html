Command terms

The command term tells you what the examiner wants. Definitions are from the IB ESS guide glossary; “in your answer” says what that means for a Paper 2 essay.

Part c markbands

Part c (9 marks) is marked against these markbands; parts a and b are marked against marking points. The bands are the same at SL and HL except that HL answers must include HL lenses content.

How examiners apply them

  • Best fit: a response can match different rows at different levels; the mark reflects the overall balance.
  • You do not need every descriptor in a band to be awarded a mark in it.
  • Within a band, the lower mark means the response only just fits; the upper mark means it fits well.

How to write a high-scoring essay

Each heading is a 7–9 markband descriptor. The HL and SL requirements are almost identical; the only difference is that HL answers must include content from the HL lenses.

A structure for part c

1. Introduce

Define the key terms in the question and outline the issue. Use lots of terminology.

2. Break it down

Unpack the big idea, e.g. sustainability into environmental, social and economic domains. Build the argument that links the concepts.

3. HL lens

Introduce the ethical, economic or legal lens you will use, then weave it through your examples.

4. Examples

About three named examples showing breadth by topic, by region, or preferably both. If time is short, do one example well.

5. Counter-arguments

Argue several viewpoints. Use EVSs to look at different perspectives.

6. Conclude

Refer back to your examples and give an explicit judgement. At HL, include the ethical or economic arguments that support it.
